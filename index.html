What we build

Core system

Lead Conversion System

Everything between "new lead" and "closed deal", wired to run in seconds instead of days:

  • Speed to lead — automated first-touch the moment a lead arrives, plus instant alerts to your team
  • Qualification & routing — structured intake that sorts real buyers from tire-kickers before anyone picks up the phone
  • Booking & show-up — calendars, reminders and nurture that get prospects on the call and keep them from ghosting
  • Pre-call intelligence — your closers walk in knowing who they're talking to and what they want

Add-ons — layer on what your pipeline needs:

  • Pipeline hygiene — CRM stages rebuilt to match how you actually sell, so forecasts mean something
  • Email & nurture sequences — leads who aren't ready yet stay warm instead of going dark
  • Old-lead reactivation — the leads you already paid for, worked again systematically
  • Close-friction removal — contracts, payments and handoffs smoothed so won deals don't stall at the finish line

Engagement model: one-time system build + a monthly retainer that keeps it monitored, tuned and growing with your volume.

Scale layer

Support + Knowledge Base

  • Self-updating — every solved issue feeds back in automatically, so the system gets smarter every week
  • Sales knowledge captured — your closers' routing logic and call insights, documented instead of trapped in their heads
