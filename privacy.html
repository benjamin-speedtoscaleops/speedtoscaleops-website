Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

The short version: this website collects almost nothing. We only hold personal information you choose to give us — by booking a call or emailing us — and the data our clients ask us to work with during an engagement. We don't sell data, run ads, or use tracking cookies.

1. What this website collects

  • Nothing you don't type elsewhere. The site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no analytics or tracking scripts of our own. We set no cookies.
  • The calculator runs in your browser. The numbers you enter never leave your device — nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations under its own privacy statement. We don't see or keep those logs.
  • Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google's servers and Google receives your IP address as part of serving them.

2. When you book a call or email us

Booking goes through Google Calendar's appointment page, so the name, email address and anything else you enter there is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy and shared with us to run the call. If you email admin@speedtoscaleops.com, we keep the correspondence. We use this information to prepare for and follow up on your audit, and for nothing else. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't share or sell your details.

3. Data we handle for clients

Our work involves connecting to a client's CRM, enquiry forms, calendars and messaging. Those systems contain personal information about the client's leads and customers — names, contact details, enquiry history. For that data:

  • The client owns it and decides what happens to it; we act only on the client's instructions, under the engagement's service agreement.
  • We use it solely to build and run the client's systems — never for our own marketing, never sold, never shared beyond the tools the client has chosen.
  • Access is limited to the team members doing the work, and we remove our access when an engagement ends.
  • If you are a lead or customer of one of our clients and have questions about your data, the client is the right first contact; we'll support any request they pass to us.

4. Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to admin@speedtoscaleops.com and we'll respond within 30 days. Deleting booking data held by Google may also require acting in your own Google account.

5. Retention and security

We keep correspondence and call notes for as long as we might work together, and delete them on request. Client-system access runs through the client's own tools and their permission controls, protected by strong authentication on our accounts.

6. Children

Our services are for businesses. The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Changes and contact

If our practices change, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions about privacy go to admin@speedtoscaleops.com.
